### Projects

- [Smart Teacher Assistant](https://github.com/Jean-Mardini/Smart-Teacher-Assistant): multi-agent LLM platform that generates summaries, slides, quizzes and grading rubrics from course documents. I was the technical lead (architecture, LangGraph orchestration, FastAPI layer). Python, FastAPI, LangGraph, FAISS.
- [Acoustic anomaly detection](https://github.com/Jean-Mardini/acoustic-anomaly-detection): detecting faulty machines from their sound under domain shift (DCASE 2024, MIMII-DUE). Frozen BEATs embeddings + GMM reach 0.638 mean AUC against ~0.55 for autoencoders. I trained the models. PyTorch, scikit-learn, FastAPI, Docker.
- [Plant disease detection](https://github.com/Jean-Mardini/plant-disease-detection): leaf disease classification trained on lab images and tested on field photos, from ~30% to 75.4% accuracy on PlantDoc. I worked on model training and part of the Flask app. PyTorch, ResNet50, EfficientNet.
